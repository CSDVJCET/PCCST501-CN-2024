Network Management (SNMP) & Physical Layer

Prescribed References: Forouzan (Ch 7 & Ch 9) • 9 Contact Hours

1. Network Management Framework: SNMP & ASN.1

Network management is based on the tripartite framework: SMI (Structure), MIB (Data Hierarchy), and SNMP (Protocol Operations).

SNMP Operations & PDUs (UDP Ports 161 & 162)
  • GetRequest & GetNextRequest: Manager retrieves variable values or walks OID trees.
  • SetRequest: Manager alters configuration settings on managed agent.
  • Trap: Agent initiates asynchronous alert to Manager (Port 162) on critical events.
ASN.1 and BER (Basic Encoding Rules)

BER encodes data in [Tag - Length - Value (TLV)] format ensuring cross-platform architecture independence.

2. Physical Layer: Data, Signals & Capacity Theorems

Theoretical Channel Rate Limits
Nyquist Bit Rate (Noiseless Channel)

$$\text{Bit Rate} = 2 \times B \times \log_2(L) \quad \text{[bps]}$$

$B = \text{Bandwidth (Hz)}$, $L = \text{Discrete voltage levels}$.
Shannon Capacity (Noisy Channel)

$$C = B \times \log_2(1 + \text{SNR}) \quad \text{[bps]}$$

$\text{SNR}_{dB} = 10 \log_{10}(\text{SNR}_{linear})$.

3. Digital Line Coding & Multiplexing

Scheme Voltage Rule Key Features
NRZ-L $0 = +V$, $1 = -V$ Simple; DC component and sync loss on long 0s/1s.
Manchester $0 = +V \to -V$, $1 = -V \to +V$ Self-synchronizing; requires double bandwidth.
AMI (Bipolar) $0 = 0V$, $1 = \text{Alternating } +V/-V$ Zero DC bias; loss of clock sync on long 0s.
Multiplexing Techniques
  • FDM: Analog frequency division with guard bands (Radio/CATV).
  • WDM: Optical wavelength multiplexing in single-mode glass fiber.
  • TDM: Digital time-slot interleaving (Synchronous vs Statistical TDM).
