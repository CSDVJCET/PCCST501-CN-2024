Module 1 CO1 (Understand) • 6 Contact Hours

Application Layer & Peer-to-Peer Paradigms

Overview of the Internet, protocol layering (TCP/IP vs OSI), Application layer services, Client-Server model, HTTP/1.1 & HTTP/2, FTP dual-channel, SMTP/MIME, DNS hierarchy, and BitTorrent P2P swarming.

Module 2 CO2, CO3 (Apply & Analyze) • 18 Contact Hours

Transport & Network Layer Architecture

Process-to-process delivery, UDP & TCP, 3-way handshake, AIMD Reno congestion control, Berkeley Socket API, I/O Multiplexing (`select()`), IPv4/IPv6, CIDR Subnetting, Routing: OSPF Link State & RIP Distance Vector, QoS, and Linux kernel routing stack.

Module 3 CO4 (Apply & Design) • 11 Contact Hours

Data Link Layer, MAC Protocols & Wireless LANs

Node-to-node framing, Bit/Byte stuffing, CRC-32 Modulo-2 division, Hamming (7,4) error correction, MAC protocols (ALOHA, CSMA/CD, CSMA/CA), IEEE 802.3 Ethernet, IEEE 802.11 Wi-Fi, Mobile IP, and Linux `PF_PACKET` raw socket sniffer.

Network Management & Physical Layer Fundamentals

SNMP, SMI, MIB-II tree, ASN.1 & BER encoding, Data & Signals, Attenuation, Nyquist Bit Rate, Shannon Channel Capacity, Digital Line Coding (Manchester, AMI, NRZ), Multiplexing (FDM, TDM), and Transmission Media.
